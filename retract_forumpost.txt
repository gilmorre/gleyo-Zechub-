What is Gleyo?
Gleyo lets communities and creators fund quests that reward contributors in shielded ZEC. The core idea is no-wallet-upfront onboarding: newcomers can complete tasks and earn ZEC before ever dealing with wallet setup. That removes the biggest cold-start friction point for everyday users entering the Zcash ecosystem.

What's been delivered (November 2025 to August 2026)

Mainnet live: Launched during ZecHub Hackathon 2026, live at gleyo.app
Real traction: 38+ weekly active users processing real mainnet ZEC flows, deposit, quest reward, shielded withdrawal, end to end
Cross-ecosystem impact: Merged an upstream fix to Nozy Wallet (PR #180) resolving an Axum routing incompatibility during the July 28 Ironwood upgrade, restoring compatibility for Gleyo and every other project depending on Nozy's API
