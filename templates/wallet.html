Get a Zcash wallet

Rewards from Gleyo are sent in ZEC. Zodl is a shielded Zcash wallet — here's how to install it and get set up in a few minutes.

Screens below are shown on iPhone. Zodl looks the same on Android — only the system bar at the top and share menu look a little different.
01

Download Zodl

Search for Zodl on the App Store or Google Play, or use the buttons below.

App Store Google Play

Zodl on the App Store.

02

Create your wallet

The first time you open Zodl, you'll be asked to create a new wallet. Tap through to generate one — this only takes a few seconds.

First screen on open.

03

Back up your recovery phrase

This is the most important step. Your recovery phrase (also called a seed phrase) is the only way to restore your wallet if you lose your phone — so don't skip it.

Tap the menu icon in the top right → More → Advanced Settings → Recovery Phrase. You may be asked to confirm your PIN first.

Watch: Finding and backing up your recovery phrase
Recovery phrase walkthrough
Keep this phrase safe
  • Write it on paper, in order — never a screenshot, photo, or notes app.
  • Store it somewhere private, away from your phone.
  • Never share it with anyone — not Zodl, not Gleyo, no exceptions.
  • Anyone with these words can access your funds. There's no recovery if it's lost or leaked.
04

Copy your shielded address

Once your wallet's set up, head to the receive screen and copy your shielded address — this is what you'll add to Gleyo so rewards can be sent to you.

Tap the copy icon.

Home screen after setup.

You're all set

Paste your shielded address into your Gleyo profile, and rewards will be sent there directly.
