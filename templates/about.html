ZEC-Native Community Growth

Gleyo is the first quest and retention platform built on Zcash — reward contributors privately in ZEC, track real retention, and build long-term community engagement.

ZEC-Powered Quest System

Admins create structured quests with ZEC reward pools. Users complete tasks and withdraw rewards directly to their shielded Zcash wallet — non-custodial, no middleman.

Shielded Rewards

All payouts are processed as shielded Orchard transactions via Nozy Wallet API. Users connect with Zodl, Zkool, Zingo!, or any Zcash wallet that supports Orchard addresses.

Built-in Community Chat

Unlike Zealy's detached Discord/Twitter approach, Gleyo has a native community space where admins can reward active members directly in ZEC — no external platform needed.

Real Retention Analytics

Track dropoff rates, retention percentages, and actionable growth signals — not vanity metrics. Know exactly why users leave and what keeps them, with risk alerts and growth opportunity insights.

Zcash Wallet Verification

Create an account, connect a shielded Zcash wallet, and verify ownership using a privacy-preserving memo transaction. Unlock ZEC-powered rewards, withdrawals, and community participation.

The Problem

Most community platforms optimize for spikes, not sustainability. Engagement fades after campaigns end, rewards are custodial and traceable, and analytics show vanity numbers that don't tell you why users leave.

Our Approach

Gleyo is built ZEC-native from the ground up. Rewards go directly to shielded Zcash wallets — no custodian, no other token. Analytics focus on real retention data: dropoff rates, growth opportunities, and risk alerts that actually help you act.

Built on Zcash

Powered by Zebra (Zcash full node) and Nozy Wallet API by LEONINE DAO for shielded Orchard transactions. Every reward, every interaction stays within the Zcash privacy umbrella.

Our Mission

To make community growth measurable, sustainable, and privacy-first — with ZEC as the native currency of contribution.
