Connect wallet

Your Zcash wallet is where your rewards are sent. Verify it once and you can join communities and receive ZEC.

Overview

Gleyo needs a Zcash wallet that supports Unified shielded addresses. Compatible wallets include Zodl, Zkool, and Zingo.

Basics

Verify your wallet

  1. Create your account.
  2. Open your Profile, then Account Settings.
  3. Choose Wallets in the left sidebar.
  4. Click Verify with Zcash and enter your Zcash unified address.

Once verified, your account is ready and you can explore communities and quests right away.

Tip

Your unified address starts with u1. Copy it straight from your wallet to avoid typos.

FAQs

A Zcash unified address that starts with u1. Transparent t-addresses are not supported.

No. You only provide your address. Community managers use it to send your rewards.

Any wallet that supports unified shielded addresses, including Zodl, Zkool, and Zingo.

What is a quest

Quests are one of the main ways to get involved on Gleyo. Complete tasks, earn XP, and climb the leaderboard. Some quests also come with ZEC rewards.

Overview

A quest is a structured mission with a clear goal and a set of tasks. Almost every quest awards XP when you finish it. A smaller number also pay out ZEC on top of that. Some quests take minutes, others unfold over several sessions. Browse the quests inside each community.

Basics

Tasks and rewards

A quest is made of up to 5 tasks. When every task is approved, the quest is complete and you earn its XP. Only some quests have a ZEC reward. When one does, it becomes available to withdraw once the quest is completed.

Review types

Some tasks are verified automatically, while others need manual approval from community admins.

FAQs

It depends on the quest. Some take minutes, others unfold over several sessions.

Tasks that need manual approval wait in the admin’s review queue. You’ll get a notification once your submission is approved or rejected.

X (Twitter)

Verify social actions on X automatically.

Overview

X tasks come in three kinds: Follow a profile, Like & Repost a post (you can also ask for a reply), and join a Space. Gleyo checks the action on X for you.

Basics

Before you start

Contributors must connect their X account in their settings before they can complete an X task. Without it, the task can’t be verified.

For admins: set up the task

  1. Add an X (Twitter) task to your quest.
  2. Pick a mode: Follow, Like & Repost, or Space.
  3. Paste the link: a profile link for Follow, a post link for Like & Repost, or a Space link for Space.
  4. For Like & Repost, choose what to ask for: Like, Retweet, and Reply.

Gleyo loads a preview of the profile, post, or Space so you can confirm it’s the right one. If the link can’t be found, you’ll see an error and need to fix the link before you publish.

Note

If you ask for a reply, contributors paste the link to their own reply on the task.

Claim credit costs

Every claim has a base cost of 1 credit. X tasks add extra credits on top of that base cost, depending on the action you ask for.

X task or actionAdditional cost
Follow+1 credit
Space+1 credit
Reply+1 credit
Retweet+2 credits
Like+3 credits

On a Like & Repost task, the actions you turn on are additive. Each one you select adds its own cost. If none are selected, the task adds nothing. The extra credits count toward your community’s claim usage, the same as the base cost.

Examples

A quest with one Follow task costs 1 + 1 = 2 credits per claim. A quest with one Like & Repost task asking for Like, Retweet, and Reply costs 1 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 7 credits per claim.

Note

Costs add up across tasks. If a quest has more than one X task, the extra cost of each one is added to the 1-credit base.

How contributors complete one

  1. Connect your X account in your settings.
  2. Do the action on the right profile, post, or Space.
  3. Come back and claim the quest. Give it a few seconds, since Gleyo checks the action on X.

For Space tasks, the button changes with the Space: Join Space Now when it’s live, Set Reminder before it starts, and Space Ended once it’s over.

Claim button disabled?

Click the link button on any link task in the quest, such as Follow on X, and the Claim button turns on.

Basics

Turn it on

  1. Click on the quest.
  2. On the right is the quest settings.
  3. Mark the task as auto-approved.
Heads up

Auto-approved tasks are not checked by hand. Use it only when proof doesn’t matter.

Tasks that are verified automatically

Tasks that can be verified automatically, like X (Twitter), Discord, YouTube, Telegram, GitHub, and Proof of Humanity, are checked by the platform itself. They are validated automatically from the start, so the auto-approval option is hidden and you can't turn it on or off for them.

Tasks with their own validation switch

URL, Text, Number, and Puzzle tasks have an Automatic validation switch inside the task. Turn it on and the claim skips review. Turn it off and each submission waits in the queue. File uploads always need review unless auto-approval is on.

Short, competitive bursts with a start date, an end date, and prizes.

Overview

A sprint is a time-boxed competition. Contributors earn XP from the quests inside the sprint and climb the leaderboard.

Basics

Who wins

Contributors at the top of the sprint ranking win the reward tiers set for the sprint. Only quests added to the sprint count.

Not every sprint is paid out by Gleyo. Each sprint is either distributed by Gleyo or self-distributed:

Note

Basics

XP

Quests can also award XP, which builds your reputation within the community.

Reward methods

Admins choose how each reward is given out:

  • All: everyone who completes the quest gets the reward.
  • FCFS: the first people to finish get it, up to a max supply.
  • Raffle: completers enter a draw, and winners are picked when the quest ends or reaches max contributors.
  • Vote: completers are voted on by other community members.

ZEC rewards use FCFS, so a ZEC reward has a max supply of winners.
